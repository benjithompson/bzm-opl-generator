# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, and taking the other person's constraints as real
- Disagreeing about the work rather than about the person
- Accepting a correction gracefully, and giving one specifically
- Saying what you actually measured, and what you only assume

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, an
  account id, a cluster hostname — without their explicit permission
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## A note specific to this project

Issues here quote real infrastructure. When you paste a log, a manifest, a
`profile.json` or an evidence file, **redact what identifies an account or a
customer** — AUTH_TOKENs, API keys, harbor and ship ids, internal hostnames,
account names. Nobody will mind an over-redacted paste; the reverse is somebody
else's data on a public page.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/benjithompson/bzm-opl-generator/issues) for
anything public, or privately via
[a security advisory](https://github.com/benjithompson/bzm-opl-generator/security/advisories/new)
if it should not be. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
